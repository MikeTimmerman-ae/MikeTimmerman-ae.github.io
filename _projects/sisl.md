highlight: "Development of a training framework allowing autonomous vehicle to perform well when subjected to a range of driving behaviours of the surrounding cars. When training in an environment with conservative cars, the autonomous vehicle becomes over-aggressive, failing when it encounters another aggressive car. When training in an environment with aggressive cars, the autonomous vehicle becomes too conservative itself. The developed training framework adjusts the training distribution towards more challenging driving behaviors and uses the importance sampling ratio to de-bias the rewards."
